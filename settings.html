Legal Technology Prototype Notice

The Court Evidence Management System (CEMS) is an academic and technological demonstration prototype. It executes on a client-side architecture using Browser Web APIs and LocalStorage, designed to simulate modern enterprise LegalTech SaaS platforms. It is completely backend-ready via its decoupled api.js REST abstraction layer. This prototype does NOT claim government certification, production judicial authority, or official forensic accreditation.

Demo Prototype — This application stores demonstration data locally in the browser. It is not intended for production court evidence handling, legal record management, or official chain-of-custody purposes.
